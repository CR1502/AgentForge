# Helix Dynamics — Customer Support SLAs

This page defines the service-level agreements (SLAs) for Helix Dynamics customer
support. SLAs vary by support tier and by issue severity.

## Support Tiers

Helix Dynamics offers three support tiers: **Bronze, Silver, and Platinum**.
There is no "Gold" tier. Every paying customer is assigned exactly one tier.

## Severity Levels

Issues are classified into three severity levels:

- **Sev-1 (Critical):** production outage or data loss.
- **Sev-2 (Major):** significant degradation, no workaround.
- **Sev-3 (Minor):** general questions and cosmetic issues.

## First-Response Time Targets

| Tier     | Sev-1      | Sev-2      | Sev-3        |
|----------|------------|------------|--------------|
| Bronze   | 8 hours    | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| Silver   | 2 hours    | 8 hours    | 1 business day |
| Platinum | 15 minutes | 1 hour     | 4 hours      |

## Coverage Hours

Bronze support is available during business hours (9am–6pm Eastern, Monday to
Friday). Silver adds extended hours until 10pm Eastern. **Platinum support is
available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, including holidays.

## Dedicated Contacts

Platinum customers are assigned a named **Technical Account Manager (TAM)** and a
dedicated Slack Connect channel. Silver and Bronze customers use the shared
support portal.

## Uptime Commitment

Helix Dynamics guarantees **99.95% monthly uptime** for Platinum customers and
**99.9%** for Silver. Bronze has no contractual uptime guarantee. Service
credits for missed uptime are capped at 25% of the monthly fee.
