# Helix Dynamics — Company Overview

Helix Dynamics is a privately held infrastructure software company. This page
records the canonical company facts used across the internal handbook.

## Founding and Leadership

Helix Dynamics was founded in 2017 in Asheville, North Carolina. The company's
headquarters remain in Asheville at 44 Spindle Row, Suite 300.

The current Chief Executive Officer is Mara Velasquez, who co-founded the
company. The Chief Technology Officer is Dev Okoro, and the Chief Financial
Officer is Priya Nandakumar.

## Size and Structure

As of January 2026, Helix Dynamics employs 612 people across four offices:
Asheville (HQ), Austin, Lisbon, and Singapore. The company operates on a fiscal
year that begins on April 1.

The engineering organization is divided into three pillars: Relay Platform,
Data Services, and Developer Experience. Each pillar is led by a Vice President
who reports directly to the CTO.

## Mission and Products

Helix Dynamics builds real-time event-routing infrastructure. Its flagship
product is **Helix Relay**, a message-routing gateway. A secondary product,
**Helix Atlas**, provides observability dashboards for Relay deployments.

## Official Colors and Branding

The company's primary brand color is "Helix Teal" (hex `#0FA3A3`). The internal
wiki, called **The Coil**, is the authoritative source for all policies. When
this handbook and another document disagree, The Coil takes precedence.

## Annual Company Event

Every year Helix Dynamics hosts an internal conference called **CoilCon**, held
during the second week of October in Asheville. Attendance is mandatory for all
full-time engineers.
