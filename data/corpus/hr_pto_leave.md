# Helix Dynamics — PTO & Leave Policy

This page defines paid time off (PTO) and leave for all Helix Dynamics
employees. It is maintained by the People Operations team.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue **22 days** of PTO per year. PTO accrues monthly at a
rate of 1.833 days per month and is available for use immediately upon accrual.

Employees may carry over a maximum of **10 unused PTO days** into the next
calendar year. Any balance above 10 days on December 31 is forfeited.

PTO requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least **14 calendar days** in
advance through the Coil Leave Portal. Requests shorter than 3 days require
48 hours' notice.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Each employee receives **12 sick days** per year.
Unused sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required for any sick
leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Helix Dynamics offers **16 weeks** of fully paid parental leave to all new
parents, regardless of gender, available within the first 12 months after birth
or adoption. Parental leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks.

## Bereavement and Jury Duty

Bereavement leave is **5 paid days** per immediate family member. Jury duty is
covered in full for up to 10 business days per year.

## Company Holidays

Helix Dynamics observes **12 paid company holidays** per year, plus two
"floating holidays" that employees may use on any day of their choosing.

## Unlimited Policy Clarification

Helix Dynamics does **not** offer an unlimited PTO policy. The accrual-based
system described above is the only PTO model in effect.
